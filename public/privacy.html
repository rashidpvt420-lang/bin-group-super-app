BIN GROUP ("we," "our," or "us"), operating as All Kind Building Projects Contracting L.L.C S.P.C, trading as BIN GROUP L.L.C - S.P.C (trade licence CN-5914744, Abu Dhabi), is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy applies to all users of the BIN GROUP Super App, including property owners, tenants, technicians, brokers, and administrators.

تلتزم شركة All Kind Building Projects Contracting L.L.C S.P.C, trading as BIN GROUP L.L.C - S.P.C (رخصة تجارية رقم CN-5914744، أبوظبي) بحماية خصوصيتك. تنطبق هذه السياسة على جميع مستخدمي التطبيق بما فيهم الملاك والمستأجرون والفنيون والوسطاء والمشرفون.

1. Who This Policy Covers / نطاق السياسة

This policy covers all five user types within the BIN GROUP platform (property owners, tenants, technicians, brokers, administrators):

  • OWNER Property owners managing portfolios, contracts, and financial records.
  • TENANT Tenants submitting maintenance requests and managing tenancy.
  • TECHNICIAN Field technicians receiving job dispatches and GPS tracking during active duty.
  • BROKER Property brokers managing referrals and commissions.
  • ADMIN Internal BIN GROUP operations staff.

2. Information We Collect / المعلومات التي نجمعها

We collect the following categories of personal information:

  • Identity Data: Full name, email address, phone number, and Emirates ID number (for KYC verification).
  • Identity and Business Documents (KYC): Images or files you upload to verify your identity or authority, such as an Emirates ID, trade or business licence, ownership documents, and tenancy or other contracts. These are stored in access-restricted storage and shown only to authorised BIN GROUP staff and the roles that need them for your request.
  • Sign-in Data: If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account name, email address and profile photo from Google. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Messages and Chat: Messages you send through in-app chat and support channels, including any attachments, so that the people involved in your request can respond to you.
  • Broker Data: For brokers: lead and referral details, attribution records, commission records, and the documents needed to verify a broker account.
  • Location Data: (a) Technicians: real-time GPS coordinates during active duty, used for job dispatch, arrival evidence and route optimization; tracking stops when the technician ends their shift. (b) Owners: during property onboarding the app can read your device location, with your permission, to help set or confirm a property's position. You can decline the permission and enter the address manually.
  • Financial Data: Payment references, approved Cash/Cheque evidence, invoices, receipts, and payroll/rent-related records required for the service. Card processing is not enabled for current Owner activation; if a card provider is enabled under a future approved policy, card details will be handled by that provider rather than stored by BIN GROUP.
  • Property Data: Property details, maintenance history, photos, and contract records associated with your account.
  • Usage Data: App interactions, feature usage, error logs — used to improve service quality.
  • AI Feature Inputs and Outputs: Text, photos and documents you submit to AI-assisted features (for example the AI assistant, AI Design Studio, damage assessment and document reading) and the results generated for you. See section 6 for the providers involved.
  • Photos/Media: Before-and-after maintenance photos uploaded by technicians or tenants as part of the Evidence-Vault™ workflow.
  • Device Data: Device type, OS version, push notification token for service alerts.

3. How We Use Your Information / كيفية استخدام المعلومات

  • Authenticate your identity and provide role-appropriate access to the platform.
  • Match and dispatch the nearest available certified technician to your property.
  • Process rent payments, maintenance invoices, and technician payroll.
  • Generate digital property records, contracts, and compliance reports.
  • Send push notifications for job assignments, ticket updates, and payment confirmations.
  • Fulfill UAE regulatory obligations (RERA, DLD, WPS).
  • Verify identity and documents (KYC) for owners, brokers and technicians.
  • Provide AI-assisted features you choose to use. AI output is a suggestion and is reviewed by people before any approval, payment or property record is changed.
  • Improve and secure the platform through aggregated analytics.

4. GPS & Location / نظام تحديد المواقع والموقع

Continuous GPS tracking applies to technicians during active duty hours. Owners can share a one-time device location during property onboarding. Technician location data is:

  • Written to Firebase Firestore every 10 seconds while a technician is on an active job.
  • Visible only to the assigned property owner and BIN GROUP operations staff.
  • Automatically stopped when the technician ends their shift or completes all jobs.
  • Never sold or used for advertising. It is processed by the service providers listed in section 6 only to run the platform.

5. Camera & Photo Access / الوصول إلى الكاميرا والصور

Camera access is requested when you take a photo, for example when a technician or tenant submits a maintenance request or completes a job, or when an owner or broker photographs a document. Photos are uploaded to Firebase Storage, encrypted at rest, and accessible only to the parties involved in the ticket (tenant, assigned technician, property owner, and BIN GROUP admin).

6. Third-Party Services / الخدمات الخارجية ومزودو الذكاء الاصطناعي

We use trusted third-party services and third-party AI processors to operate the platform (cross-border transfers require explicit consent):

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication, database, cloud storage, and push notifications.
  • Google Maps: Google Maps Platform supports property address lookup, maps and technician route surfaces where the required provider configuration is enabled.
  • OpenAI: Processes text and images submitted to AI-assisted features (for example the AI assistant, design studio and damage assessment) through our backend when that feature is configured. Requests are sent from BIN GROUP servers, not directly from your device. This is a third-party AI processor and may involve cross-border transfer, only with explicit consent.
  • Google Gemini (Google): Processes text and documents submitted to AI-assisted features (for example the AI assistant, property description analysis and document OCR) through our backend when that feature is configured. This is a third-party AI processor and may involve cross-border transfer, only with explicit consent.
  • Payment providers: Current Owner activation uses Cash/Cheque evidence. Card processing is not enabled for current Owner activation. Stripe or another card provider is used only if separately enabled and verified under the active payment policy.

Images you upload to the AI Design Studio and the designs generated from them are stored in private storage linked to your account and are shown only to you and authorised BIN GROUP staff.

We do not sell your personal data to any third party.

7. Data Retention / الاحتفاظ بالبيانات

We retain data according to its configured retention class and applicable legal, contractual, operational, and audit requirements, including the 10-year immutable signed-contract audit trail. Signed contracts and associated identity verification data are retained for 10 years for statutory compliance. You may request deletion of eligible account data as described on the account deletion page. Request deletion of your data where legally permitted. These periods are for counsel to confirm.

Account deletion: you can ask us to delete your account either inside the app (More options menu → Request account deletion) or by email, without signing in. We confirm that the request comes from you, then complete the deletion within 30 days. Some records, such as contracts, invoices, payment and audit records, may have to be kept for the period required by law (including the 10 years signed-contract retention) or for the resolution of a dispute. Full steps, what is deleted and what may be kept are on our Account Deletion page (https://bin-groups.com/delete-account).

8. Data Security / أمن البيانات

We implement role-based access control (RBAC), Firestore security rules, Firebase App Check, and TLS encryption for all data in transit. Sensitive fields (Emirates ID, IBAN) are access-restricted to authorised roles only.

9. Your Rights (UAE PDPL) / حقوقك بموجب قانون حماية البيانات

Under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021, for counsel to confirm), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data where legally permitted.
  • Withdraw consent for optional data processing.

To exercise these rights, contact: support@bin-groups.com

To request deletion of your account and data, see How to delete your account.

10. Children's Privacy / خصوصية القاصرين

BIN GROUP is intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

11. Changes to This Policy / التعديلات

We may update this Privacy Policy as the platform evolves. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
