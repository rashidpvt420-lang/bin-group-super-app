This page explains how to request deletion of a BIN GROUP account for All Kind Building Projects Contracting L.L.C S.P.C, trading as BIN GROUP L.L.C - S.P.C, trade licence CN-5914744, Abu Dhabi, headquartered in Al Ain, United Arab Emirates. You do not need to sign in to read this page or to send a request by email.

توضح هذه الصفحة كيفية طلب حذف حساب BIN GROUP الخاص بـ All Kind Building Projects Contracting L.L.C S.P.C, trading as BIN GROUP L.L.C - S.P.C، الرخصة التجارية CN-5914744, Abu Dhabi ومقرها في العين، الإمارات العربية المتحدة.

1. Request account deletion / كيفية طلب حذف الحساب

Option A — inside the app (signed-in users):

  1. Open the BIN GROUP app and sign in to your account (Owner, Tenant, Technician or Broker).
  2. Open account or profile settings.
  3. Tap Request account deletion, add an optional note, and submit the request.

Option B — by email (no sign-in needed): Send an email to ceo@bin-groups.com from the email address registered on your account with the subject "Account deletion request – BIN GROUP". Include your full name and registered role.

سجّل الدخول إلى التطبيق وافتح إعدادات الحساب أو الملف، ثم اختر حذف الحساب (Request account deletion) أو راسل ceo@bin-groups.com من بريدك الإلكتروني المسجَّل.

2. Identity verification / التحقق من الهوية

Identity verification ensures that the request originates from the legitimate account holder before deleting access credentials and personal data that is no longer required.

يتم التحقق من الهوية (Identity verification) للتأكد من أن الطلب صادر من صاحب الحساب الفعلي قبل حذف بيانات الاعتماد.

3. Timeline (30 days) / المدة الزمنية (30 يوماً)

Once identity is confirmed, account deletion requests are reviewed and processed within 30 days.

بعد التحقق من الهوية، يُنفّذ حذف الحساب خلال 30 days (30 يوماً).

4. What is deleted / ما يتم حذفه

Details regarding What is deleted:

  • Login credentials, profile data, personal preferences, and device push notification tokens.
  • Operational GPS tracking logs not retained as evidence of completed maintenance work.
  • Uploaded photos and messages not part of an immutable contract, payment, safety, or legal record.

يشمل ما يتم حذفه (What is deleted) بيانات تسجيل الدخول والملف الشخصي والرموز المميزة للجهاز وسجلات الموقع غير المرتبطة بإثبات عمل منجز.

5. What may be retained / ما قد يتم الاحتفاظ به

Details regarding What may be retained and why:

  • The signed-contract audit trail (IP address, device identifier, server timestamp, and SHA-256 hash of accepted terms) retained for 10 years, which is immutable under UAE law.
  • Financial evidence, invoices, payment records, Cash/Cheque receipts, rent records, and payroll data required for statutory accounting and tax compliance.
  • Maintenance, safety, and incident tickets and dispute evidence required to protect legal rights and fulfill duty of care.
  • Compliance and KYC records required under UAE Anti-Money Laundering statutes.

يشمل ما قد يتم الاحتفاظ به (What may be retained) مسار تدقيق العقود الموقعة لمدة 10 years وسجلات الفواتير والدفع وإثباتات الصيانة ومتطلبات مكافحة غسل الأموال.
