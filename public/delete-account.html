Delete Your BIN GROUP Account

Last Updated: October 28, 2026

This page explains how to ask for your BIN GROUP account and personal data to be deleted. The BIN GROUP app (Android and iOS) and website are operated by All Kind Building Projects Contracting L.L.C S.P.C, Al Ain, United Arab Emirates. You do not need to sign in to read this page or to send a request by email.

1. How to request deletion

Option A — inside the app (signed-in users):

  1. Open the BIN GROUP app and sign in to your account (Owner, Tenant, Technician or Broker).
  2. Open the More options menu (three dots) in the top bar.
  3. Tap Request account deletion, add an optional note, and tap Submit account deletion request.

Option B — by email (no sign-in needed): send an email to ceo@bin-groups.com with the subject "Account deletion request – BIN GROUP". Write from the email address registered on your account and include your full name and your role (Owner, Tenant, Technician or Broker). Please do not send passwords, ID numbers or card numbers by email.

2. Identity verification

To protect your account, we confirm that the request really comes from you before deleting anything. In-app requests are tied to your signed-in account. For email requests we check that the message comes from the email address registered on the account, and we may ask you to confirm details only you would know (for example the phone number or property linked to the account) by replying to that same address. Requests we cannot verify will not be processed.

3. How long it takes

We acknowledge your request and, once your identity is verified, complete the deletion within 30 days. We will email you when it is done, or tell you if any information must be kept and why.

4. What is deleted

  • Your account login and profile: name, email address, phone number, profile photo and language/notification preferences.
  • Device and notification data, including push notification tokens.
  • Location data collected for technician dispatch and job tracking, where it is not needed as a record of completed work.
  • Photos, documents and messages you uploaded that are not part of a contract, payment, safety, or dispute record.

You can also ask us to delete only some of your data (for example, uploaded photos or your phone number) without closing your account. Email ceo@bin-groups.com and say which data you want removed.

5. What may be retained, and why

Some records must be kept even after your account is deleted, because UAE law and our contracts require it, or to protect people and prevent fraud:

  • Contracts, tenancy and service agreements, and their digital signatures.
  • Invoices, receipts, payment references, Cash/Cheque evidence, rent and payroll records, and other financial records needed for accounting, tax and audit.
  • Maintenance, safety and incident records and dispute evidence (for example completed-job photos and tickets).
  • Identity (KYC) and compliance records that we are legally required to keep.
  • Security and audit logs needed to detect fraud and prove what happened.

Retained records are kept only for the period required by law, contract or the resolution of a dispute, are restricted to authorised BIN GROUP staff, and are not used for marketing. Where possible we remove or de-identify personal details inside them. After the retention period ends they are deleted.

Deleting your account cannot be undone. If you are an Owner with active contracts, a Tenant with an active tenancy, or a Technician or Broker with unpaid balances, we may need to settle these before closing the account, and will tell you what is outstanding.
